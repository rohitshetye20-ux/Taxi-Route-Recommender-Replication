# Research Background

Urban taxi drivers often experience:

- High passenger search time
- Increased fuel consumption
- Traffic congestion
- Low operational efficiency

The original research proposes a Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) combined with Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) to recommend profitable taxi routes using both spatial and temporal information.

---

# Problem Statement

Objective:

Develop and reproduce an intelligent taxi route recommendation system capable of predicting high-demand destinations.

Key challenges include:

- Capturing spatial dependencies

- Predicting future passenger demand
- Improving driver profitability
- Maintaining computational efficiency
