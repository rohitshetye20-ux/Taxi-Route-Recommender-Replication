# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers, pledge to make participation in our project and community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, inclusive, diverse, and healthy community.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Prioritizing the overall success of the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Insulting, abusive, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Project documentation
- Community forums
- Any official communication channels related to this repository

It also applies when an individual is officially representing this project in public spaces.

---

# Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying and enforcing acceptable standards of behavior.
- Taking fair and appropriate corrective action when necessary.
- Reviewing reported incidents objectively.
- Maintaining a welcoming environment for contributors.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening a private GitHub Issue or contacting the project maintainer through GitHub.

When reporting an incident, please include:

- A description of the issue.
- Relevant screenshots or links (if available).
- Steps or context leading to the incident.
- Any additional information that may help with the investigation.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

---

# Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will review each reported incident individually.

Possible responses include:

### 1. Correction

A private, written warning requesting that the inappropriate behavior stop.

### 2. Warning

A formal warning explaining the violation and expectations for future participation.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

Temporary suspension from project interactions.

### 4. Permanent Ban

Permanent removal from participation in the project for repeated or severe violations.
